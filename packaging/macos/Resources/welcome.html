PeerLoom Seeder

Keeps your PeerLoom apps in sync while your phones sleep.

It runs in the background on this Mac, also when nobody is logged in, and stores only scrambled data it cannot read. A new phone still needs someone in the household online to join.

When the install finishes, the dashboard opens in your browser. Pair a phone from there. You can open it again any time from PeerLoom Seeder in Applications.
