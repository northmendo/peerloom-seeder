# PeerLoom Seeder: listing copy

For the Umbrel and Start9 stores, the website page and the GitHub releases.
Suite rule 13 applies to release notes; this is the standing description.

## Name

PeerLoom Seeder (Umbrel and Start9 id `peerloom-seeder`)

## Tagline

Keeps your PeerLoom apps in sync while your phones sleep.

## Short description (one line, store cards)

An always-on copy of your PeerLoom apps' data, on a box you own, so your
devices catch up even when nobody else is online.

## Description

Your PeerLoom apps sync phone to phone. When everyone's phones are asleep or
out of signal, nothing moves until two of them are online together. PeerLoom
Seeder fixes that. Run it on a computer or home server that stays on, pair
your phones with it once, and it keeps a copy of your shared lists, files
and spaces for them. A phone that wakes up catches up from the seeder, even
if the person who made the change is offline.

One seeder serves every PeerLoom app: PearList, PearOffice and the apps that
come after them. You own it, it runs on your hardware and you decide who
pairs with it.

It stores your data scrambled and cannot read it. It never gets the keys to
your spaces, so what it holds is useless to anyone who takes the box. What
its owner can see is how many spaces each paired device keeps there, how big
they are and when they change.

It does not replace people. A new phone or a new member still needs someone
already in the household or group to be online once to let them in. After
that, the seeder keeps them in sync.

## What you get

- Pair a phone by scanning a code on the dashboard
- See each paired device with its app, the spaces it keeps here and the space
  they use
- Rename a device, or forget one so the seeder stops storing for it
- A storage limit per device (25 GB to start, change it or turn it off)
- Runs on Umbrel, Start9, Linux, macOS and Windows

## Requirements

A machine that stays on and online. A few hundred MB of disk for lists and
documents; more for big files.
